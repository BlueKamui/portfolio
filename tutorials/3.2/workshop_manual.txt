SPOKE AND SPROCKET: WORKSHOP HANDBOOK

A fictional bike workshop, invented for this course. None of these prices, names or policies are
real, so no model can know them from its training.

# Opening hours and booking

The workshop is open Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 to 17:30. We are closed on Sundays and Mondays, and
those days do not count as working days. Book by phone or at the counter. We do not take bookings
by email. Drop your bike off before 10:00 on the booked day. A bike that arrives after 10:00 moves
to the next free slot.

# Service levels and prices

We offer three service levels. The Safety Check costs 35 pounds and takes about 45 minutes while
you wait. It covers brakes, tyres, headset play and a short test ride in the yard.

The Full Service costs 119 pounds. It includes everything in the Safety Check, plus a full
drivetrain clean, gear and brake cable adjustment, wheel truing, and new brake pads if the old
ones are worn past our limit. Parts other than brake pads are charged on top.

The Strip-down Overhaul costs 229 pounds. Every bearing is taken apart, cleaned and regreased, and
all inner cables and outer housing are replaced.

# How long a service takes

A Full Service is ready at 16:00 on the third working day after drop-off. An Overhaul takes five
working days. If we find a fault that needs a part we do not stock, we phone you before ordering
it, and the clock stops until the part arrives.

# Chain wear

We measure chain wear with a checker gauge at every service. At 0.5 per cent stretch we recommend
a new chain, 28 pounds fitted for an 11-speed chain. At 0.75 per cent the cassette has usually
worn with it, and we replace both together for 85 pounds fitted.

# Skipping under load

If the chain skips across the sprockets when you stand on the pedals, but shifts cleanly when you
spin gently, the cause is almost always a worn chain and cassette, not the gear adjustment. We
check wear first, then the alignment of the rear hanger, and only then the cable tension.
Adjusting the cable on a worn drivetrain hides the problem for about a week.

# Brakes

Rim brake pads are replaced when less than 1.5 mm of pad material is left, and disc brake pads when
less than 1 mm is left. We bleed hydraulic brakes only with the fluid the manufacturer specifies.
Mineral oil and DOT fluid must never be mixed: each one destroys the seals made for the other. A
bleed costs 25 pounds per brake.

# Tubeless tyres

Tubeless sealant dries out. We top it up every six months, or at any service, for 8 pounds per
wheel. A tyre that will not seat on the rim is refitted with new rim tape for 15 pounds per wheel.

# Electric bikes

We service the brakes, gears and wheels of electric bikes at the normal prices, plus 15 pounds
because of the extra weight. We do not open motors or batteries. Those go back to the dealer of the
brand that made them. Remove the battery before you drop the bike off, and keep it at home.

# Loan bikes

A loan bike is available during a Full Service or an Overhaul, for a refundable deposit of 50
pounds. There are four loan bikes, all hybrids with a medium frame, so book one when you book the
service.

# Collection and storage

We send a text message when your bike is ready. Bikes that are not collected within 14 days of that
message are charged 3 pounds per day for storage.

# Warranty on our work

Our labour is guaranteed for 90 days. If a cable we adjusted slips back within that time, we
adjust it again for free. Parts carry the warranty of the company that made them, not ours.

# Wheel building

We build wheels by hand to order. A pair takes four weeks from the day the rims and hubs arrive,
and starts from 320 pounds. Every wheel is re-tensioned free of charge after the first 300 km.
